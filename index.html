Our Story

A family kitchen that just kept growing

Dapur Sekar started as a small warung on Jalan Teuku Umar, with six tables and one wok. Ibu Sekar cooked the dishes she grew up with in Gianyar, and people kept coming back.

Ten years on we have a bigger kitchen, but the approach hasn't changed: market-fresh ingredients, spice pastes ground by hand, and portions that leave you full.

Our base genep is still made from scratch every morning, with eleven spices pounded on a stone mortar. It takes time, but it's the reason our betutu tastes the way it does.

We work with rice farmers in Tabanan and fishermen in Jimbaran, and we keep the menu short so everything we serve can be made properly, every day.

    Bought from the market every morning, never frozen.

  • Authentic Recipe
